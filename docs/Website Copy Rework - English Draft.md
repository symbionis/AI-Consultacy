# Website Copy Rework - English Draft

**Date:** 19 May 2026
**Scope:** Cards 1-3 ("What this is, in three parts") + Step 3 ("Coaching Together") in "How we start"

For each section: the current copy, then the proposed replacement.

---

## Card 1 - 01 / Understanding

### Current

> **A humanistic diagnostic.**
>
> I offer you a real conversation about your univers, what you're carrying, what's been waiting, and where you want to make more room. The whole activity, the whole person. Not the tools, but the things that keep coming back and weighing on you.
>
> You leave with a clear read of where you stand. And two experiments to try this week.

### Proposed

> **A real conversation about how you actually work.**
>
> We start from your week: what took your time, what only moves when you're the one doing it, what keeps coming back. Then we look at what's been waiting. The project you haven't launched. The decision you haven't had time to think through. The writing that recurs but never gets easier.
>
> You leave with a clear read of where you stand, and two experiments to try this week.

**What changed:** Dropped "humanistic diagnostic" (positioning says keep it internal). Replaced abstract language with the concrete, factual register from Discovery V3: last week, real tasks, recurring patterns, waiting projects. Mirrors the actual phases of the conversation without naming them. Fixed the "univers" typo by removing it entirely.

## Card 2 - 02 / Continuity

### Current

> **Six sessions, alongside you.**
>
> A journey of six weekly sessions, alongside you. We get to know how you actually operate, what you're carrying, and where to start. Then we shape it together and refine it until the weight lifts.
>
> A project you've been carrying moves forward. Your mental load lifts.

### Proposed

> **Six sessions. Real things built.**
>
> Six weekly sessions where we shape Claude around how you actually work. We give it your voice, your way of thinking, your tone. We launch your first workspace for the area that matters most, and start crafting skills for the actions you repeat every week.
>
> By the end, a project you've been carrying has moved forward. The weight starts to lift.

**What changed:** Replaced the vague "journey" language with concrete deliverables: Claude shaped to your voice, a first workspace, skills for recurring actions. Names Claude directly. Keeps the emotional payoff (weight lifts, project moves forward) but earns it with specifics.

## Card 3 - 03 / A system you keep

### Current

> **Yours, after we stop working together.**
>
> I leave you with a working system shaped to your operation, one you understand and can evolve. Your own workflows, your own routines, custom skills that work alongside you.
>
> If our engagement ends, your proposals still draft themselves. Your Monday brief still arrives.

### Proposed

> **Yours, after we stop working together.**
>
> Claude knows your voice principles. Your skills handle the work that used to wait on you. Your workspace runs the way you think, not the way a generic AI thinks you should.
>
> Things move: the follow-ups, the weekly brief, the draft that arrives and just needs sending. Things ship: the offer that's been at 80% for six months, the case study sitting in a folder unused. It compounds, and it's yours to keep.

**What changed:** Replaced "system" language with concrete use cases drawn from the positioning doc's "what stops waiting on them" and "the promise, framed humanely" sections. The closing uses three short assertions (things move / things ship / it compounds) instead of the previous talkative sentence. Use cases are broad enough for any entrepreneur to recognise something in them, specific enough to land.

## Step 3 - Coaching Together (in "How we start")

### Current

> **Coaching Together, if we both want to continue.**
>
> Six weekly sessions. We shape a working system around how you actually operate, your own workflows, your own routines, custom skills that work alongside you. One project you've been carrying brought visibly forward.
>
> *Currently accepting a small number of first-cohort clients through summer 2026.*

### Proposed

> **Coaching Together, if we both want to continue.**
>
> Six weekly sessions. Here's what we build:
>
> **Claude, shaped to you.** We give Claude a personality that matches yours: your tone, your instincts, your way of thinking. It remembers how you work, so every interaction starts from understanding, not from scratch.
>
> **Your voice, recognised.** We define your voice principles so that what Claude writes for you sounds like you wrote it. Proposals, emails, client notes, all in your register.
>
> **A workspace for your area.** We launch your first working environment around the area that matters most to your operation, whether that's client work, content, hiring, or admin.
>
> **Skills for your recurring work.** We craft dedicated skills for the actions you repeat: drafting proposals, preparing briefs, following up, summarising meetings. The things that take your time and shouldn't.
>
> **Good practice that sticks.** You learn how to work with Claude well. The habits, the shortcuts, the extra tools that make the interaction feel natural rather than technical.
>
> One project you've been carrying, brought visibly forward.
>
> *Currently accepting a small number of first-cohort clients through summer 2026.*

**What changed:** Broke the vague single paragraph into five named deliverables matching the brief. Each one is concrete and names what the client walks away with. Kept the emotional anchor (one project brought forward) and the launch scarcity signal. This is the section where someone is deciding whether to commit: specificity does the selling here.

## Notes for review

- **Cards 2 and 3 hint, Step 3 spells out.** The cards give you enough to understand the shape. The Coaching Together section gives you the full list. This avoids the cards feeling like a feature dump while still making the commitment feel substantial.
- **Claude is named throughout.** This makes the offering tangible. If you later broaden beyond Claude, these sections update easily: the structure holds, you just swap the name.
- **Vocabulary stays clean.** No banned words. "Weight," "voice," "carrying," "waiting" all present. "Humanistic" removed from public copy. No "productivity," "automation," "transform."
- **The Discovery card now mirrors V3.** Someone reading it will arrive at the session expecting exactly what happens: a conversation grounded in their actual week, their patterns, their waiting projects. No mismatch.
